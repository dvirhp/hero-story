# Story review rubric (separate from automated QA)

Use synthetic requests and native-language reviewers. Record request fixture,
profile/rubric/prompt versions, manuscript identifier, reviewer language expertise,
page-specific evidence and uncertainty. Do not store children's identifying data
in review reports. Read every page aloud; inspect the complete book, not excerpts.

Score each dimension 1 (unacceptable), 2 (major revision), 3 (minor revision),
4 (ready for editorial acceptance). Averages cannot override a safety concern or
incorrect teaching. Proposed editorial gate: every dimension at least 3, no
unresolved safety/education issue, independent second review for disagreements.
This threshold is a QA proposal requiring product/editorial approval.

| Dimension | Human evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Literary quality | Natural prose, coherent transitions, satisfying closure and emotionally reassuring tone; identify awkward lines |
| Developmental suitability | Vocabulary, sentence complexity, density, pacing and read-aloud rhythm fit the selected provisional profile; babies may have sensory sequences without conflict |
| Narrative variety | Distinct page contributions and imagery; distinguish useful refrains from filler; assess agency appropriate to age without imposing one plot template |
| Educational accuracy | Correct concepts, examples and target order; Hebrew final forms explained accurately with appropriate word-final examples; one meaningful teaching focus per page |
| Personalization | Name preserved naturally, supplied pronouns consistent, child participates meaningfully; no gender inference |
| Language and culture | Fluent native phrasing, Hebrew agreement/niqqud, logical RTL order, culturally appropriate treatment and no stereotypes |
| Safety and emotional experience | No harmful instruction, frightening escalation, shame, prejudice or misleading factual claims; review context beyond literal safety matches |

Review short and long books across newborn, infant, toddler, preschool and older
profiles in both currently supported languages. Include both densities, alphabet,
adventure, bedtime and educational requests. Global language support is a product
vision: additional languages need native reviewers and approved implementation.

Automated schema checks, script ratios, word counts, literal target inclusion and
synthetic Fake Provider acceptance prove none of these subjective qualities.
No paid manuscript generation is authorized by this rubric.
